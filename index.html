Spread Pick 'Em

Beta · Live square, three designs

One square per player, as you chose, drawn three ways. Same three players and five games in each: two finished, one live, two not kicked off. Green is always the spread pick and brown the winner pick, as on the Picks tab. Swipe each grid sideways, then tell Claude 1, 2 or 3.

Each square is split in two: spread pick on the left, winner pick on the right. Each half turns green or red on its own.
