Spread Pick 'Em

Beta · Live tab, new approaches

Two new ways to show everyone's picks, and Option A from before for comparison. Same three players and five games in each: two finished, one live, two not kicked off. Tell Claude which one.

New 1 · Game cards with names

No grid. The Live tab uses the same card as the Picks tab, and each box lists who made that pick. A box turns green or red when the game is graded.

New 2 · One call per square

A grid, but each square is a single call. One logo means that team all the way: the favorite to cover, or the underdog to win. Two logos mean the underdog covers but the favorite wins. After the game the square shows the points earned.
